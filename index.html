How it works

One bomb. One rule.
Don't be holding it.

Every round is short, loud and chaotic. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you spawn.

🏟️1

Drop into the arena

All players spawn together in a blocky arena. Get moving and find some space. The round is about to start.

💣2

The bomb is lit

One random player gets the bomb and the fuse starts burning. Nobody knows exactly when it will go off.

🖐3

Slap to pass it

Holding the bomb? Chase someone down and slap them. The bomb jumps to them, and now they're the one panicking.

💥4

BOOM. Last one wins

When the fuse runs out, whoever holds the bomb is out. New bomb, new round, until one survivor is left.

The rules & pro tips

  • Never stand still. A player standing still is an easy target for a slap.
  • Watch the fuse. The closer it is to zero, the more desperate everyone gets.
  • Use the map. Walls, ramps and corners can save you, or trap you.
  • Slap fast, run faster. Right after you pass the bomb, get away before it comes back.
  • Last one standing wins the match. Then you do it all again.

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is Slap or Bomb free?

Yes. It's completely free to play on Roblox. Just open the game and hit Play.

What devices can I play on?

Right now Slap or Bomb is PC only (Windows and Mac). We are working on mobile, and it will come in a future update.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Join the same server as your friends on Roblox and start slapping the bomb at each other.

What does "Open Beta" mean?

The game is playable now, but still being built. Expect updates, new content and the occasional bug. Your feedback shapes what comes next.

How do I report a bug?

Tell us what happened, what you were doing and which device you played on. A screenshot helps a lot.
